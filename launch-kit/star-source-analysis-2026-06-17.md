## What Worked

1. Measured artifacts with receipts: benchmark pages, losses pages, reproducible comparisons, security audits, and concrete fixes.
2. Honest negative framing: "where Sverklo loses", "grep is better here", "the fix that was not", "late rerank made F1 worse".
3. Agent-specific pain: Claude Code hallucinated names, tool overload, token spend, Codex CLI wiring, Windows setup.
4. Product releases tied to those pains: proof command, profile reduction, doctor/init improvements, Codex support, real-user fixes.
5. Founder account technical posts when they included a specific measured claim, not generic promotion.

## What Has Not Worked Yet

1. Generic proof asks posted into the void.
2. Directory/listing PRs as a short-term engagement engine. They are credibility infrastructure, not a star source by themselves.
3. Empty or low-distribution brand-account posting.
4. Star-goal framing. It does not give a maintainer a reason to run anything.

## Double-Down Rule

Do not spend the next sprint on "more places to post".

Spend it on artifacts a skeptical maintainer can inspect, falsify, or argue with:

- a benchmark delta,
- a reproduced failure,
- a visible loss,
- a grep-better case,
- a same-task before/after receipt,
- a product fix shipped from a correction.
